About the Boutique

A neighbourhood boutique for every occasion on your calendar

Located in Sector 15A, near Radha Swami Satsang Road, Vrinda Creations is a women's clothing boutique serving families across Faridabad — from everyday ethnic wear to the outfit you wear on the biggest day of your life.

Pick a ready piece off the rack, or bring your own fabric and design — our in-house stitching turns it into a fit that feels made only for you.

  • Designer suits, sarees & lehengas for festive and wedding wear
  • Custom stitching & made-to-measure fitting
  • Open all seven days, 10 AM to 10 PM
